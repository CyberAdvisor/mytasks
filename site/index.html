Before you rely on it

My Tasks is ready for use, within its limitations. Please read these before you put anything important in it:

  • You need a GitHub account and a personal access token. Your tasks are kept, encrypted, in a secret gist in your account. (A gist is a file stored in your GitHub account. A secret gist isn't listed or searchable, but anyone with its web address can open it, which is why My Tasks encrypts it.)
  • A forgotten passphrase can't be recovered. Without it, your tasks can't be decrypted.
  • If you edit the same task on two devices before they sync, the later edit wins and the other is lost.
  • No reminders or notifications, no widgets, no Siri. Only your last change can be undone.
  • Android is best effort.

Everything in its place

A clear structure for everything you need to do.

Today, and what's next

Inbox, Today, Scheduled, Unscheduled and Deferred. Tasks roll into Today when their day comes; deadlines are flagged in red.

Areas, projects, headings

Group work into areas and projects, split projects with headings, and add checklists and notes to any task.

Priorities

Five priority levels with names you choose. Due and overdue tasks can get P1 automatically.

Contexts

Mark where or how a task gets done — Errands, Phone, Computer — and filter Today by it.

Group Today your way

See Today as one list, or grouped by project, priority or context. Save searches to the sidebar.

Made for the keyboard

Quick Entry, Quick Find and a full set of keyboard shortcuts, wherever the browser allows them.

Free, private, secure

No server of ours ever sees your tasks.

No subscription, no ads, no tracking. The code is on GitHub under the MIT licence.

How it works

About five minutes on your first device; one minute on each one after that.

Open the app

Go to mytasks.fyi/app in Safari, Chrome or Edge.

Connect GitHub

Create a token with the gist permission and paste it in. Step by step

Choose a passphrase

It encrypts your tasks. Write it down: it can't be recovered.

Install it

Add it to your Home Screen or Dock, then connect your other devices the same way.
