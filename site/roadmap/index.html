Roadmap

Last updated November 14, 2026

What’s planned for My Tasks, grouped by milestone and the version it’s planned for. Plans can change; items marked research are being looked into, not promised.

Coming next

Milestone 8 · Tidy and polish v1.1

  • Collapsible areas, and collapsible headings in projects
  • Empty-list illustrations, a new logo, and a sidebar you can resize
  • Phone gestures: pull down to search, swipe from the edge to go back
  • Print a list, and a count of Today’s tasks on the app icon
  • A full check against the design, and testing on iPhone and iPad
  • Sequential projects: when a task is done, the next one moves into Today, with a priority and context you choose
  • Automated tests of the interface
  • On this website: an About page, a guide to hosting or changing your own copy, a way to follow updates, and setup guides for PARA, GTD and other methods

Milestone 9 · Calendar events v1.2

  • Today’s and coming events from your Mac’s calendars, shown in Today and Scheduled on all your devices, through a small helper you run on your Mac
  • Click an event to open it in Calendar

Milestone 10 · Power editing v1.3

  • Select several tasks and change their priority, context or project, or delete them
  • Repeating tasks
  • Type dates in plain words, such as “next fri”
  • Undo and redo more than one change
  • Drag tasks onto the sidebar’s lists and between Today’s groups, and swipe actions on phones
  • Nested tags, tag shortcuts, and speed with very long lists

Milestone 11 · Your data v1.4

  • Switch between gists, for example one for work and one for personal tasks
  • Import from Things, Todoist and others
  • Other places to keep your tasks (research)

Milestone 12 · Sharing v2.0

  • Small teams sharing a gist, with an assigned field on tasks (research)

Completed

Milestone 7 · Ready for release v0.7.1 to v1.0.0

  • An independent review of security and privacy, and its fixes
  • Back up and restore your tasks, in Settings

Milestone 6 · Finishing touches v0.4.1 to v0.7.0

  • Typing straight into a new task, and a final Empty Trash
  • This website, with its guide, limitations, privacy and security pages
  • Confirming the risks and limitations at setup, and links to this website from the app

Milestone 5 · Sync, install and hosting v0.1.0 to v0.4.0

  • Light and dark themes, and the Completed list
  • Encrypted sync between your devices through your own GitHub gist
  • Install as an app that works offline, hosted at mytasks.fyi

Milestone 4 · Drag and drop, keyboard and undo v0.1.0 to v0.2.2

  • Drag and drop in lists and the sidebar, keyboard shortcuts, and undo
  • Each task’s right-click menu, and the menus after each list’s title
  • Contexts, and grouping Today by project, priority or context

Milestones 1 to 3 · The foundations v0.1.0

  • Inbox, Today, Scheduled, Unscheduled, Deferred, Completed and Trash
  • Areas, projects, headings, tags, checklists, notes, dates and deadlines
  • Priorities, saved searches, search and Quick Entry
