Before you start

  • A free GitHub account. My Tasks keeps your tasks, encrypted, in a secret gist there: a file in your account that isn't listed or searchable.
  • Safari on iPhone or iPad (iOS 18.4 or later) or on a Mac (macOS 14 or later), or Chrome or Edge on Mac, Windows or Linux. Android works but isn't tested.
  • About five minutes for your first device.

Install My Tasks

Open mytasks.fyi/app. Installed, My Tasks opens in its own window, works offline and keeps its data safe. On iPhone and iPad, install it before you connect: the Home Screen app keeps its own copy, separate from Safari.

DeviceHow to install
iPhone, iPadIn Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Open My Tasks from your Home Screen.
Mac (Safari)Choose File › Add to Dock…, then Add. Open My Tasks from the Dock.
Chrome or EdgeClick Install My Tasks at the bottom of the sidebar, or the install button at the right of the address bar.
FirefoxFirefox can't install web apps. My Tasks works in a tab, or use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Create a GitHub token

The first screen asks for a personal access token, which lets My Tasks keep your tasks in a gist in your account.

  1. Click How to get a token, then the link to GitHub's new token page, and sign in. It opens with the name "My Tasks" and the gist box ticked.
  2. Choose an expiry, such as 90 days. When it expires, the sidebar says the token isn't valid: make a new one and use Change Token in Settings › Sync on each device. GitHub also removes tokens that go unused for a year.
  3. Leave only gist ticked, then click Generate token.
  4. Copy the token (it starts with ghp_) and paste it into My Tasks.
  5. Read the risks and limitations, tick the box that says you have, and click Connect.

A token with the gist permission can read and change all of your gists, so give it an expiry. It stays on your device and is used only with GitHub.

Choose a passphrase

On your first device, choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters and type it twice. A password manager, or four random words, makes a good one. My Tasks encrypts your tasks with it before anything leaves your device, and creates a secret gist named "My Tasks".

Write it down or keep it in a password manager. It can't be recovered. If you forget it and no device is still connected, your data is lost.

Add another device

  1. Open mytasks.fyi/app on the device and install it.
  2. Paste a token: the same one, or a new one made the same way.
  3. My Tasks finds your "My Tasks" gist. Enter your passphrase.

Anything already on that device is merged in. From then on, each device syncs a few seconds after every change, and when you open it.

Everyday use

  • Lists. Inbox holds new tasks. Today shows what's due today, Scheduled what's coming, Unscheduled what's open with no date, and Deferred what you've set aside. Completed and Trash hold the rest.
  • Adding tasks. Use the + button, or press N or Space in a list. Quick Entry (Ctrl Space) adds one to the Inbox from anywhere in the app.
  • When and deadlines. Give a task a day and it moves to Today when that day comes. A deadline shows the days left and turns red when it's due.
  • Areas, projects and headings. Create them with New List at the bottom of the sidebar. Drag tasks and projects to rearrange them. The arrow beside an area in the sidebar, or a heading in a project, hides or shows what’s under it; your other devices keep the same.
  • Priorities. Set P1 to P5 on any task. In Settings you can rename the levels, and turn off the automatic P1 for due tasks and P2 for tasks added to Today.
  • Contexts and tags. Add contexts (where or how something gets done) in Settings, and tags on any task. The bar under each list's title filters by them.
  • Today grouping. Click the ⋯ after the Today title and choose Group By: All, Projects, Priorities or Contexts.
  • Saved searches. Search with Quick Find, then Save Search to keep it in the sidebar.
  • Completed tasks move to Completed daily by default. Change it in Settings › Completed Items.
  • Undo. Press Cmd Z to undo your last change. On touch devices, tap Undo in the message that follows a delete or move.
  • Sync. The bottom of the sidebar shows when your tasks last synced. Settings › Sync has Sync Now, Change Passphrase and Disconnect This Device.

Back up and restore

Your gist is where every device's tasks are kept; a backup file is a separate copy that you keep yourself.

  • Save Backup in Settings › Backup downloads a file of your tasks, projects, areas, tags, contexts, saved searches and synced settings. On iPhone and iPad, choose Save to Files in the share sheet. The file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe.
  • Restore from Backup… replaces all your tasks with the file's, on this device, in your gist and, once they sync, on your other devices. Anything created since the backup is deleted. It can't be undone, so the confirmation offers Save Backup First.
  • Restore needs a working sync connection. Your theme, token, passphrase and gist connection don't change.

Troubleshooting

You seeWhat to do
Token not valid, or it is missing the gist scope.Create a new token with only gist ticked, then use Change Token in Settings › Sync.
Offline — changes saved on this deviceNothing: your changes are kept and sync when GitHub can be reached again.
Passphrase changedThe passphrase was changed on another device. Click Enter it and type the new one.
Your tasks are too large to upload.Your changes are still on this device and your gist is unchanged. Remove what you don't need, such as long notes, then use Sync Now in Settings › Sync.
This token belongs to a different GitHub account, or can't reach your gist.Make the token in the GitHub account that owns your gist. If the gist was deleted, use Disconnect This Device in Settings › Sync and set it up again.
Your My Tasks gist was not found.It may have been deleted on GitHub. Click Recreate Gist to make it again from the tasks on this device.
This file isn't a My Tasks backup, or the backup is damaged.Choose a file saved with Save Backup. Nothing was changed.
Restore needs a working sync connection.Fix the problem shown in Settings › Sync, or wait until you're online, then restore again. Nothing was changed.
Update availableClick Restart to load the new version. What you're typing is saved first.
Your tasks were saved by a newer version of My Tasks.Reload the app to update it.
A list looks different on iPhone and in SafariThe installed app and Safari keep separate copies. Use the installed app.
