Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

My Tasks has no server and no accounts. The app doesn't send your tasks, your passphrase or your token to the project. This page explains what is stored, where, and who can see it.

What is stored, and where

WhereWhatWho can read it
Your device, in the browser's storageA working copy of your tasks and your settings, so My Tasks works offline. Separately: this device's GitHub token, the gist's id and the encryption key.Anyone who can use your unlocked device. It is not encrypted.
Your GitHub account, in a secret gist named "My Tasks"Your tasks, encrypted with your passphrase. Only the app's name, the file format, the last-updated time and the values needed to decrypt it with your passphrase (salt and IV) are in plain text.Someone with your passphrase. A secret gist isn't listed, but anyone with its web address can download the encrypted file. GitHub can see that the gist exists, its size, when it changes, and the details above that are in plain text.
A backup file, wherever you save itOnly if you use Save Backup: your tasks and synced settings. Never your token, your passphrase or the gist's id.Anyone who can open the file. It is not encrypted.

What My Tasks doesn't collect

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies, in the app or on this website.
  • No accounts with the project, and no email address.
  • The app connects only to itself and to GitHub's gist API. Its Content Security Policy blocks every other connection.

Other services

  • GitHub stores your gist and sees your requests to it, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the app. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they're signed in to GitHub (GitHub's documentation).

Deleting your data

  • From a device: Settings › Sync › Disconnect This Device removes that device's list, token and key. Your gist is kept.
  • Everywhere: delete the "My Tasks" gist at gist.github.com, revoke the token at github.com/settings/tokens, then disconnect each device.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. To report a security problem privately, see Security.
